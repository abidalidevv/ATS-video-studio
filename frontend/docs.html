1. Engine Superpowers & Architectural Highlights

Traditional AI video generators suffer from slow API download queues, multi-pass re-encoding artifacts, and generic repetitive stock footage. Avatar Storyteller Engine revolutionizes the workflow through 7 core technical innovations:

📂 Local B-Roll Footage Pool Engine

Reads directly from your local SSD/NVMe drive with zero internet latency. Automatically shuffles non-repeating clips until the total duration satisfies the audio length, adding a 4-second safety buffer and muting native stock audio (-an).

👤 Author Avatar Cutout & Glowing Stroke

Upload any cutout PNG or JPG portrait of your storyteller/host. Automated Pillow alpha mask dilation draws a razor-sharp outer glow border (Clean White, Champagne Gold, Electric Cyan) ensuring the avatar stands out with punch against dark stock backgrounds.

🌫️ 16:9 Cinematic Blur & Dark Tint Overlay

Dual-slider control applying boxblur (0–30px) and dark dimming tint (0–80%) exclusively to the stock footage layer. The Avatar portrait, Subtitles, and Visualizer remain 100% razor-sharp, crisp, and unblurred on top.

💬 Smart Adaptive Kinetic Subtitles

Subtitles automatically compute their margin boundaries to position themselves in the open space opposite the avatar (Avatar Left ➔ Subtitles Right; Avatar Right ➔ Subtitles Left). Includes zero-overlap clamping (next_start - 0.04s) eliminating LibASS vertical line stacking.

🎙️ Audio DSP Studio & BGM Ambience

Integrated voice pitch shifting (-4st deep radio broadcaster bass to +4st high tone), tempo adjustment (0.8x – 1.25x), and dedicated background music mixer with audio ducking and :normalize=0 to preserve voiceover punch.

⚡ Single-Pass GPU Turbo Renderer

Executes video concatenation, slow-motion speed retiming, background blur, dark tint, avatar compositing, visualizer animation, and ASS subtitle burning in one single FFmpeg pass. Auto-detects NVIDIA NVENC (h264_nvenc), Intel QSV, AMD AMF, or CPU fallback.

2. Standalone Portable Executable & Friend Sharing Guide

100% Zero-Setup for Recipients

The engine features an automated one-click batch compiler that packages the entire application into a completely portable, standalone Windows release: dist/ATSAuthor-Windows-Portable.zip (~185 MB).

How to Build the Standalone Package:

  • 1
    Double-click 3_BUILD_NEW_Standalone_EXE.bat in the project folder.
  • 2
    The automated script verifies Python, checks/installs PyInstaller, cleans previous builds, bundles all 12 viral TTF fonts, bundles static portable bin/ffmpeg.exe and bin/ffprobe.exe, copies frontend UI assets, and compiles ATSAuthor.exe.
  • 3
    The script compresses the entire release folder into dist/ATSAuthor-Windows-Portable.zip.

How Your Friend / Client Runs It:

  • 1
    They extract ATSAuthor-Windows-Portable.zip anywhere on their PC (e.g. Desktop or D:\Drive).
  • 2
    They double-click ATSAuthor.exe (or Launch-ATSAuthor.bat).
  • 3
    The studio opens instantly in an ultra-clean, full-featured desktop window. They do NOT need Python, FFmpeg, or any third-party software installed!
🛡️ Self-Healing Dynamic Output Directory

When settings are shared, if data/settings.json points to an output folder that existed on your development machine (e.g., C:\Users\Abid\...), the portable engine automatically detects that the folder does not exist on your friend's PC and seamlessly falls back to data/output/ inside their portable directory. No errors, no missing directory crashes!

4. Studio 5-Step Production Workflow

Step 1: 🎙️ Voiceover Audio & BGM Ambience

Upload your narration voiceover (.mp3, .wav, .m4a, .aac, .ogg). The file input uses zero-copy URL.createObjectURL for instant 0ms preview playback.

  • Voice Pitch Shifter: Real-time slider from -4st (deep broadcaster voice) to +4st (youthful bright tone) using rubberband/asetrate filters.
  • Voice Tempo Slider: Accelerates or slows pacing (0.8x – 1.25x) without altering pitch.
  • Background Music (BGM): Upload an ambient track. Audio ducking scales BGM volume down to 7% with :normalize=0 so narration punch remains 100% intact.
Step 2: 🖼️ Author Avatar & Glow Stroke

Upload an author cutout portrait (PNG with transparency or JPG).

  • Dock Position: Choose Left, Center, or Right.
  • Outer Stroke Glow: Select Clean White, Champagne Gold, or Electric Cyan. An alpha mask dilation filter computes a smooth, high-contrast border.
  • Flip Horizontal: Mirrors the author portrait to face towards the subtitles.
Step 3: 📂 Local B-Roll Pool & Atmospheric Blur

Click [📂 Browse Local Folder] to select any directory containing stock video clips (MP4/MOV).

  • Stock Slow-Mo Multiplier: Slow down footage (0.5x, 0.75x, 0.85x, 1.0x) via monotonic setpts for calm, hypnotic storytelling flow.
  • 16:9 Selective Blur Radius: Adjust from 0px (sharp) to 30px (deep bokeh blur). Blurs *only* the background footage layer!
  • Ambient Dark Tint Overlay: Dimming darkness slider (0% to 80%) to maximize subtitle readability.
Step 4: 💬 Subtitles & Audio Visualizer

Choose typography and audio visualizer widgets.

  • 12 Kinetic Typography Presets: Word-level highlight with CapCut Yellow, Hormozi Punch Green, Neon Cyber, Ali Abdaal, Cinzel Luxury, and more.
  • Adaptive Opposite Placement: If Avatar is Left, captions automatically dock on the Right side of the 16:9 frame, avoiding any visual overlap.
  • Visualizer Widget: Select from 10 built-in templates or upload a custom green-screen motion graphics video.
Step 5: 🎬 16:9 Director Canvas & Turbo GPU Render

Full WYSIWYG 1920×1080 stage canvas.

  • Interactive Drag & Scale: Drag the Avatar, Captions Box, or Visualizer anywhere on the video stage. Resize with corner handles.
  • 20-Second Live Preview: Click [▶ 20s PREVIEW] to audition background video, slow-mo, voiceover, BGM, and synchronized subtitles in real-time.
  • 1-Click Turbo GPU Render: Click [🚀 RENDER 1-HOUR VIDEO (GPU TURBO)]. Renders in 4–5 minutes via hardware NVENC/QSV/AMF and automatically launches Windows Explorer highlighting your completed MP4.

6. Custom Green-Screen Visualizer Video & Chroma Keying

In addition to the 10 built-in procedural visualizers, you can upload any custom green-screen, blue-screen, or black-background soundwave or motion graphics video:

🎯 In-App Eyedropper Sampling

Click the eyedropper button and click anywhere on the uploaded video frame. The 1x1 optimized color sampler detects the exact hex color of the background and immediately keys it out.

🎛️ Live Tolerance & Smoothness Sliders

Adjust similarity tolerance (5% to 60%) to remove green halo spill, and adjust edge blend smoothness (0% to 30%) for clean, anti-aliased borders on stage.

FFMPEG SINGLE-PASS CHROMA FILTERGRAPH 
# Green / Blue Screen Video Chroma Key
-stream_loop -1 -i custom_vis.mp4 \
-filter_complex "[2:v]chromakey=color=0x00FF00:similarity=0.30:blend=0.08,scale=520:292,setsar=1,fps=30[vis_keyed]; \
[bg_blurred][vis_keyed]overlay=x=1200:y=680:shortest=1[comp]"

9. Troubleshooting & Technical FAQ

❓ Why did file selection take long previously, and is it fixed?

Generic MIME wildcards like accept="audio/*" force Windows Chromium to scan the entire Windows registry and shell codec handlers. We eliminated this by switching to explicit file extensions (.mp3,.wav,.m4a) and zero-copy URL.createObjectURL. File dialogs now open and load in 0ms.

❓ Will this run on an Intel or AMD GPU?

Yes. The backend's backend/config.py automatically detects your hardware in real-time. If NVIDIA NVENC (h264_nvenc) is not present, it automatically tries Intel QuickSync (h264_qsv), then AMD AMF (h264_amf), and cleanly falls back to CPU (libx264) if no hardware encoder is detected.

❓ Why don't the subtitles overlap vertically during fast speaking?

In backend/subtitle_generator.py, we enforce boundary clamping: each line's end timestamp is strictly clamped to next_start - 0.04s. This forces LibASS to erase the old subtitle before rendering the next line, completely eliminating vertical line stacking.
